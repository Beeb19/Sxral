Questions

Good to know.

How do I book?

Tap any "Book" button on this page to open WhatsApp, or message 0706 113 6203 directly. Tell us the treatment and preferred time, and we'll confirm.

Where are you located?

We're in Abuja, FCT. For our guests' privacy, we share the exact address and directions when your booking is confirmed.

How much do treatments cost?

Pricing depends on the treatment and session length. Send us a message and we'll share our current menu and rates.

Yes. Our couples ritual lets you enjoy treatments side by side in a private suite. Just mention it when you book.

What should I bring?

Just yourself. We provide fresh towels, robes and everything you need for your session.
